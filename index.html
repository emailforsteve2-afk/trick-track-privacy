Trick Track privacy policy

SealTeam Designs · Updated October 5, 2026

Trick Track is a Hearts and Spades scorekeeper made by SealTeam Designs. Games, player names, scores, history, and statistics stay on this device in a local database. They are not sent to SealTeam Designs or to Apple as part of scoring.

The app does not use advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not request access to your camera, photos, microphone, or location.

You can erase local data with Reset App in the menu (type “Reset the app” to confirm). Uninstalling the app also removes the on-device database.

Apple may collect information about TestFlight or App Store downloads under Apple’s own policies. That is separate from Trick Track’s scoring data.
