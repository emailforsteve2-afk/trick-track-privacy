Trick Track

Hearts and Spades scorekeeper · SealTeam Designs

Trick Track keeps score for Hearts and Spades on iPhone and iPad. One private group can include any number of players. For each game you pick the four people at the table, choose house rules, and keep a running score.

Scoring stays on the device. You can play a full night with the phone in airplane mode.

What you can do

  • Open Hearts or Spades and select four players from your group (regulars or guests).
  • Spades also assigns two partnerships.
  • Choose a rules preset (moon scoring, omnibus, bags, nil, mercy, and similar house rules).
  • Enter hands simply and keep cumulative scores, history, and win/loss stats.
  • Include guest players (no account) or registered accounts. Admins can promote scorekeepers and remove members.

What it does not do

  • It does not play the cards for you. You play at a real table; the app is the scorepad.
  • It does not use advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps.
  • It does not need your camera, photos, microphone, or location.

Getting started

First launch creates a private group and named players. After that, opening the app goes to game selection. Create an account only if you want it; guests still appear in history.
